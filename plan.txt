# EarnKaro Callback Testing Plan

To see exactly what data EarnKaro sends and ensure our "Bridge" works, follow these steps:

### STEP 1: Get a Live Test URL
1. Go to [Webhook.site](https://webhook.site).
2. Copy your "Unique URL" (e.g., `https://webhook.site/#!/abcd-1234...`).
3. This URL will act as your "Fake Ofera Backend" to catch the data.

### STEP 2: Configure EarnKaro
1. Log in to your EarnKaro Affiliate Dashboard.
2. Go to **Account Settings** -> **Postback/Webhook Settings**.
3. Paste your Webhook.site URL into the "Postback URL" field.
4. Choose the "HTTP GET" or "HTTP POST" method (Ofera will support both).

### STEP 3: Trigger a "Test Conversion"
1. Generate an EarnKaro Profit Link for any product.
2. Manually append a test User ID to the link: `...&s1=TEST_USER_999`.
3. Open the link in Incognito and (if possible) perform a small test purchase or use EarnKaro's "Test Postback" button if they have one.

### STEP 4: Inspect the Data on Webhook.site
Look for the following fields in the incoming request:
- `s1` or `subid1`: This should be `TEST_USER_999`.
- `amount` or `sale_amount`: The value of the purchase.
- `profit` or `payout`: The money EarnKaro made (30% of this goes to the user).
- `status`: Should be "pending" or 0.

### STEP 5: Replicate to Ofera Backend
1. Once you see the data format, use Postman or `curl` to send the same data to:
   `http://localhost:3000/affiliate/callback?s1=TEST_USER_999&amount=1000&profit=80`
2. Check your Ofera database to confirm the `cashback_transactions` table now has a entry for `TEST_USER_999`.
